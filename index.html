FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find answers to common questions about registration, age divisions, gear, and field schedules.

Registration & Eligibility

Are there residency or boundary requirements to play at VDO?
Yes, please check the boundary map to confirm. If you would like to play at VDO and you live outside the designated residential or school boundary, a waiver must be filled out. Please connect with our playeragent@valleydeoroll.com for more details
What documents are required to complete registration?
You will need proof of age (birth certificate) and online consent forms including the medical release, code of conduct, and release waivers. You can fill them out here.
I did not get a copy of my signed documents
The emails will be coming from playeragent@valleydeoroll.com with the subject Spring 2027 Documents for [Player Name] Are Ready. If you have checked your spam and still haven't received the documents after an hour, you can reach out to it@valleydeoroll.com and we can re-trigger the email.
What if I have multiple players?
Each player will need to have their own documents filled out. To initiate another form, click here. If it says Document has been signed already, click RESUBMIT. You will get a copy of your player's documents just like when you did for your other player. Keep an eye out for an email with the subject Spring 2027 Documents for [Player Name] Are Ready
Where do I upload my signed documents?
During the registration process on Sports Connect, you will be asked to Upload Your Completed _______ Form. You can click the button that says Choose File to upload the files that were emailed to you.

Divisions & Player Assessments

How is my child's division determined?
Division placement is primarily based on official Little League Age using our calculator above. For older divisions (Minors, Majors, Intermediates, Juniors), player evaluations and skill assessments are used to ensure balanced team creation.

Schedules & Equipment

What equipment does the league provide vs what families need to buy?
The league provides an official team jersey and hat. Families are generally responsible for purchasing a fielding glove, cleats, baseball pants, belt, and personal bat/helmet.
